Peptides Simplified/Epitalon

Aging & longevity · Sleep

Epitalon, simplified.

Also called Epithalon, AEDG

Early research

A four-building-block peptide famous in anti-aging circles for its effect on telomeres, the caps on your DNA.

AEDG
Size
4 amino acids
How it is taken
Injection
Status
Research compound, under FDA review

What is it?

Epitalon was designed in Russia based on an extract from the pineal gland, the part of the brain that makes melatonin.

What studies show

  • In the labIn human cells, it switched on telomerase, the enzyme that rebuilds telomeres.
  • In animalsSome mice and fruit flies lived longer in studies.
  • In peopleSmall studies in older adults, mostly from one research group, reported better melatonin rhythms.

What we don’t know yet

  • Almost all research comes from one group and has not been independently repeated.
  • Whether it extends life in people.

Side effects and cautions

  • Very little human safety data.

Is it legal?

Not FDA-approved. Reviewed for insomnia by an FDA advisory committee in July 2026.
