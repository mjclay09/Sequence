HCG, simplified.

Also called Human chorionic gonadotropin, Pregnyl, Novarel

Approved Rx

A pregnancy hormone that also tells the testes to make testosterone. It is an approved prescription for fertility and some hormone problems.

Size
Large hormone (protein), not a short peptide
How it is taken
Injection
Status
FDA-approved prescription
Available sizes
Vials: 5,000 IU

What is it?

HCG acts like LH, the brain’s signal to the testes or ovaries. In men, that means making testosterone and sperm. It is often used with testosterone therapy to keep natural production and fertility going.

Official prescribing information

Novarel and Pregnyl are FDA-approved. The official label shows how it was dosed in the trials that led to approval, along with warnings and who should not use it.

Doses on the label are for the approved product under a prescriber’s care. Talk with your doctor before starting anything.

What studies show

  • In peopleIt raises testosterone in men whose testes are working but not getting the signal.
  • In peopleIt helps preserve sperm production in men on testosterone therapy.
  • In peopleTrials found the "HCG diet" does not cause weight loss beyond the low-calorie diet itself.

What we don’t know yet

  • Best long-term approach alongside testosterone therapy.

Side effects and cautions

  • Can cause breast tenderness, mood changes and acne from higher estrogen and testosterone.
  • Not for use with hormone-sensitive cancers.

Is it legal?

FDA-approved. Available only with a prescription. The FDA does not approve it for weight loss.
