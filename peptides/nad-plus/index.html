Peptides Simplified/NAD+

Aging & longevity

NAD+, simplified.

Also called Nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide (not a peptide)

Human studies

A molecule every cell uses to make energy and repair DNA. Levels drop with age, which made it a centerpiece of longevity research. It is not a peptide.

Size
Coenzyme, not a peptide
How it is taken
Injection or IV
Status
Supplement and clinic infusion
Available sizes
Vials: 500 mg, 1,000 mg

What is it?

NAD+ shuttles energy inside your cells and fuels repair enzymes. Most research uses pills of its building blocks (NR or NMN). Injecting NAD+ directly is newer and less studied.

What studies show

  • In peopleOral building blocks reliably raise NAD+ levels in the blood of people.
  • In peopleEffects on how people actually feel or function have been small or mixed so far.
  • In animalsRaising NAD+ improved several markers of aging in mice.

What we don’t know yet

  • Whether injected NAD+ has benefits beyond pills.
  • Long-term effects of raising NAD+.

Side effects and cautions

  • Fast injections or IVs often cause flushing, chest tightness, nausea and cramping.

Is it legal?

Not an approved drug. Sold as a supplement and given in wellness clinics.
