Peptides Simplified/5-Amino-1MQ

Weight loss

5-Amino-1MQ, simplified.

Also called NNMT inhibitor (not a peptide)

Early research

A small molecule that blocks an enzyme called NNMT, which is more active in fat cells. It shrank fat in mice but has not been tested in people.

Size
Small molecule, not a peptide
How it is taken
Oral capsule or injection
Status
Research compound
Available sizes
Vials: 10 mg · Capsules: 50 mg

What is it?

NNMT is an enzyme that is more active in fat tissue, especially with obesity. Blocking it appears to make fat cells burn more energy. 5-Amino-1MQ is often grouped with peptides but is actually a much smaller molecule.

What studies show

  • In animalsObese mice on a high-fat diet lost fat mass and had smaller fat cells, without eating less.
  • In the labIn fat cells in the lab, it raised energy use and reduced fat storage.

What we don’t know yet

  • Whether any of this happens in people. There are no published human trials.
  • Safety in people.

Side effects and cautions

  • No human safety data.
  • Product quality varies widely.

Is it legal?

Not FDA-approved for human use.
