What is it?

Collagen is the main protein in skin, tendons and bone. Collagen peptides are made by chopping it into short chains. Your gut breaks them down further, and a few small pieces reach the blood.

What studies show

  • In peopleMany trials found modest improvements in skin elasticity and hydration after 8 to 12 weeks.
  • In peopleSome trials found less joint pain in athletes and people with osteoarthritis.

What we don’t know yet

  • How much of the benefit is from the collagen itself versus just eating more protein.
  • Many studies are funded by supplement makers.

Side effects and cautions

  • Generally very safe. Check the source if you have fish or other allergies.

Is it legal?

Sold over the counter as a dietary supplement.
