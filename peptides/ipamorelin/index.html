Peptides Simplified/Ipamorelin

Muscle & recovery · Sleep

Ipamorelin, simplified.

Also called GH secretagogue

Human studies

A tiny peptide that copies the "hunger hormone" ghrelin to trigger a pulse of growth hormone.

AibHNalFK
Size
5 amino acids
How it is taken
Injection
Status
Compounded only, not approved

What is it?

Ipamorelin acts on the same switch as ghrelin, a gut hormone that also tells the pituitary to release growth hormone. It is known for being more selective than older peptides like it.

What studies show

  • In peopleIt reliably causes a short burst of growth hormone in people.
  • In peopleA trial testing it to speed gut recovery after surgery did not beat placebo.

What we don’t know yet

  • Whether it improves muscle, fat or sleep in healthy adults. There are no solid trials on that.

Side effects and cautions

  • Headache, flushing and increased hunger are reported.
  • Banned in sport by WADA.

Is it legal?

Not FDA-approved. Included in the 2026 HHS plan to allow compounding with a prescription.
