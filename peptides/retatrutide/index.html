Peptides Simplified/Retatrutide

Weight loss

Retatrutide, simplified.

Also called "GLP-3", triple agonist

Human studies

An experimental weight-loss drug that copies three hormones at once. It has produced the largest weight loss seen so far in trials, but it is not approved yet.

Size
39 amino acids
How it is taken
Weekly injection (in trials)
Status
Investigational, not approved

What is it?

Retatrutide acts on three hormone signals: GLP-1, GIP and glucagon. The glucagon part may help the body burn more energy, on top of eating less.

What studies show

  • In peopleIn its first large Phase 3 trial, people on the top dose lost around 28 to 30% of their body weight on average.
  • In peopleIt also eased knee osteoarthritis pain in people with obesity in that trial.

What we don’t know yet

  • Long-term safety, since it is still in trials.
  • When, or whether, the FDA will approve it.

Side effects and cautions

  • Stomach side effects similar to other GLP-1 drugs.
  • Products sold online as "research retatrutide" are not the tested drug and have no quality control.

Is it legal?

Not FDA-approved. Only legally available inside clinical trials.
