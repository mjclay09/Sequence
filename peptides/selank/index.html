Selank, simplified.

Also called Tuftsin analog

Human studies

A calming nasal spray peptide from Russia studied for anxiety without drowsiness.

TKPRPGP
Size
7 amino acids
How it is taken
Nasal spray
Status
Research compound (Rx in Russia)

What is it?

Selank is based on tuftsin, a small immune peptide your body makes. It seems to affect calming brain chemicals like GABA.

What studies show

  • In peopleSmall Russian trials in people with anxiety reported less anxiety, similar to a common anti-anxiety drug but without sedation.

What we don’t know yet

  • Independent trials outside Russia are lacking.

Side effects and cautions

  • Generally reported as well tolerated. Nasal irritation is possible.

Is it legal?

Not FDA-approved. Included in the 2026 HHS compounding plan.
