Size

How it is taken
Oral, topical or injection
Status
Research compound, under FDA review

What is it?

KPV is the tail end of alpha-MSH, a natural hormone that helps control inflammation.

What studies show

  • In animalsIn mice with colitis, it reduced gut inflammation.
  • In the labIn cell studies, it lowered inflammatory signals.

What we don’t know yet

  • There are no real human trials yet.

Side effects and cautions

  • Unknown in people.

Is it legal?

Not FDA-approved. Reviewed by an FDA advisory committee in July 2026 for wound healing and inflammation.
