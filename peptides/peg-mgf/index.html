PEG-MGF, simplified.

Also called Pegylated mechano growth factor

Early research

MGF with a polymer called PEG attached so it lasts longer in the body. The evidence is the same thin animal and lab data as MGF.

What is it?

Plain MGF breaks down in minutes. Attaching PEG, a common trick in drug design, shields it so it can circulate longer.

Common questions

What is PEG-MGF?

MGF with a polymer called PEG attached so it lasts longer in the body. The evidence is the same thin animal and lab data as MGF. Plain MGF breaks down in minutes. Attaching PEG, a common trick in drug design, shields it so it can circulate longer.

What does the research say about PEG-MGF?

Small animal studies looked at muscle and heart repair, with mixed results.

Is PEG-MGF FDA approved?

Not FDA-approved for human use.

What are the side effects of PEG-MGF?

No human safety data. Banned in sport by WADA.
