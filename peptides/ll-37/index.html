What is it?

LL-37 is the only human member of a family of natural antibiotics called cathelicidins. It pokes holes in bacteria and helps direct the immune response. CAP-18 is the name of the larger protein it is cut from.

What studies show

  • In the labKills many bacteria, fungi and some viruses in lab dishes.
  • In peopleIn a small trial, applying it to chronic leg ulcers sped up healing compared with placebo.

What we don’t know yet

  • Whether injecting it helps anything in people.
  • Long-term safety.

Side effects and cautions

  • Too much LL-37 is linked to inflammatory skin conditions like rosacea and psoriasis.
  • Injection-site reactions.

Is it legal?

Not FDA-approved.
