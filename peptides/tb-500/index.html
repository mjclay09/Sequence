Healing & injury · Muscle & recovery

TB-500, simplified.

Also called Thymosin beta-4 fragment

Early research

A lab-made piece of a natural repair protein. It is often paired with BPC-157 for injuries.

Status
Research compound, under FDA review

What is it?

Thymosin beta-4 is a protein your cells use to move, build blood vessels and repair damage. TB-500 is a short active piece of it.

What studies show

  • In animalsIt improved wound, muscle and heart repair in animal studies.
  • In peopleThe full thymosin beta-4 protein was tested as eye drops and wound gels in people, with modest results. TB-500 itself has not been tested in people.

What we don’t know yet

  • Whether the fragment works like the full protein.
  • Long-term safety.

Side effects and cautions

  • Banned in sport by WADA; horse-racing positives are common.
  • Online product quality varies.

Is it legal?

Not FDA-approved. Included in the 2026 HHS compounding plan and reviewed by an FDA advisory committee in July 2026.
