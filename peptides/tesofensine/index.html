Peptides Simplified/Tesofensine

Weight loss

Tesofensine, simplified.

Also called Triple reuptake inhibitor (not a peptide)

Human studies

An oral appetite suppressant that works on brain chemicals like dopamine and norepinephrine. It is a small-molecule drug, not a peptide.

Size
Small molecule, not a peptide
How it is taken
Oral capsule
Status
Investigational in the US
Available sizes
Capsules: 500 mcg

What is it?

Tesofensine was first developed for Parkinson’s and Alzheimer’s. Researchers noticed people lost weight, so it was retested for obesity. It keeps dopamine, norepinephrine and serotonin active longer, which reduces hunger.

What studies show

  • In peopleIn a 24-week trial, people on the middle dose lost about 10% of their body weight, compared with about 2% on placebo.
  • In peopleIt has gone through late-stage trials in Mexico.

What we don’t know yet

  • Long-term heart safety.
  • How it compares with GLP-1 medicines.

Side effects and cautions

  • Raises heart rate and can raise blood pressure.
  • Dry mouth, trouble sleeping, nausea and mood changes are reported.
  • Can interact with antidepressants and stimulants.

Is it legal?

Not FDA-approved.
