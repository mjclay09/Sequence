CJC-1295, simplified.

Also called Modified GRF(1-29), often paired with ipamorelin

Human studies

A lab-made signal that nudges your body to release more growth hormone for days at a time.

How it is taken

Status
Compounded only, not approved

What is it?

CJC-1295 is a modified version of the body’s growth-hormone-releasing signal (GHRH), built to last much longer in the blood.

What studies show

  • In peopleIn small studies of healthy adults, one injection raised growth hormone levels several times over and kept IGF-1 higher for about a week or more.

What we don’t know yet

  • Whether higher growth hormone from CJC-1295 builds muscle or improves health in real life.
  • Long-term safety. Its drug development was stopped years ago.

Side effects and cautions

  • Flushing, water retention and tingling are reported.
  • Higher growth hormone can raise blood sugar.
  • Banned in sport by WADA.

Is it legal?

Not FDA-approved. In 2026, HHS announced plans to let licensed compounding pharmacies make it with a prescription.
