Oxytocin, simplified.

Also called Pitocin, "the bonding hormone"

Approved Rx

A nine-amino-acid hormone involved in bonding, trust and childbirth. Approved to start labor, and studied as a nasal spray for social connection and mood.

CYIQNCPLG
Size
9 amino acids
How it is taken
Injection or nasal spray
Status
FDA-approved for labor
Available sizes
Vials: 2 mg, 10 mg

What is it?

Your brain releases oxytocin during touch, closeness, sex, childbirth and breastfeeding. It helps social bonding and lowers stress responses.

What studies show

  • In peopleIt is routinely used in hospitals to start or strengthen labor.
  • In peopleNasal spray studies on trust and social behavior have had mixed results, and a large trial in children with autism found no benefit.

What we don’t know yet

  • Whether nasal oxytocin reliably helps mood or relationships in adults.

Side effects and cautions

  • Nasal use is generally well tolerated in studies.
  • Must not be used during pregnancy outside medical care, since it can trigger contractions.

Is it legal?

FDA-approved for use in labor. Other uses are off-label.
