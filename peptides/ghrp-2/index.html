Muscle & recovery

GHRP-2, simplified.

Also called Pralmorelin

Human studies

A growth hormone releasing peptide that mimics ghrelin, the hunger hormone, to trigger a strong pulse of your own growth hormone.

Injection

Research compound (diagnostic use in Japan)

Vials: 5 mg, 10 mg

What is it?

GHRP-2 switches on the ghrelin receptor in the brain and pituitary, which releases growth hormone. It is stronger than ipamorelin but less selective, so it also nudges up cortisol and prolactin.

What studies show

  • In peopleIn people, single doses reliably raise growth hormone levels, which is why Japan uses it as a diagnostic test.
  • In peopleIt also raises cortisol and prolactin, and increases hunger.

What we don’t know yet

  • Whether repeated use builds muscle or improves recovery in healthy adults.
  • Long-term safety.

Side effects and cautions

  • Increased hunger, water retention and tingling hands.
  • Can raise blood sugar.
  • Banned in sport by WADA.

Is it legal?

Not FDA-approved.
