BPC-157, simplified.

Also called Body Protection Compound 157

Early research

The most talked-about "healing peptide". It helped injuries heal faster in rats, but it has barely been tested in people.

How it is taken
Injection or oral
Status
Research compound, under FDA review

What is it?

BPC-157 is a small piece of a protein found in stomach juice. Scientists think it helps tissue repair partly by growing new blood vessels and calming inflammation.

What studies show

  • In animalsIn rats, it sped up healing of tendons, ligaments, muscles, bones and the gut lining.
  • In animalsIt protected the stomach and intestines from damage in many animal studies.
  • In peopleOnly a few tiny human reports exist, such as a small group with knee pain. None are large controlled trials.

What we don’t know yet

  • Whether it works in people at all.
  • Long-term safety. Because it helps grow blood vessels, some experts worry it could feed tumors.

Side effects and cautions

  • Quality of online products varies a lot.
  • Banned in sport by WADA.

Is it legal?

Not FDA-approved. In 2026, HHS announced plans to allow licensed compounding with a prescription, and an FDA advisory committee reviewed it in July 2026. Rules are still changing.
