Human studies

A piece of growth hormone once developed as a fat-loss drug. In people, it did not beat placebo.

Research compound

What is it?

Scientists cut out the part of growth hormone thought to break down fat, hoping to get fat loss without the other effects of growth hormone.

What studies show

  • In animalsIt reduced fat in obese mice.
  • In peopleIn trials with several hundred people, it did not cause meaningfully more weight loss than placebo, and development was stopped.

What we don’t know yet

  • Why the animal results did not carry over to people.

Side effects and cautions

  • Appeared well tolerated in trials.

Is it legal?

Not FDA-approved. Included in the 2026 HHS compounding plan.
