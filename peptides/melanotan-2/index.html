Melanotan 2, simplified.

Early research

An unapproved tanning peptide that also affects appetite and sex drive. PT-141 was developed from it.

What is it?

Like Melanotan 1, it copies alpha-MSH, but it acts on more receptors. That is why it also causes arousal and lower appetite. Researchers turned it into PT-141 to focus on libido.
