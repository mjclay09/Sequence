Peptides Simplified/Thymalin

Immune & gut · Aging & longevity

Thymalin, simplified.

Also called Thymulin-related thymus peptides

Early research

A mix of small peptides from the thymus, the gland that trains immune cells. It has been used in Russia for decades to support immunity.

Size
Mix of short thymus peptides
How it is taken
Injection
Status
Rx in Russia, research elsewhere
Available sizes
Vials: 10 mg

What is it?

Your thymus shrinks with age, which is one reason immunity weakens. Thymalin is a peptide extract from the thymus developed by Russian researchers, who also developed Epitalon.

What studies show

  • In peopleRussian studies report improved immune markers in older adults and people with weak immunity.
  • In peopleRussian long-term studies in older adults reported lower death rates, but the studies were small and not well controlled.

What we don’t know yet

  • Results from independent, well-controlled trials outside Russia.

Side effects and cautions

  • Little Western safety data.
  • Product quality varies.

Is it legal?

Not FDA-approved.
