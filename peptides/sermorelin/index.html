What is it?

Sermorelin is the shortest piece of GHRH that still works. It tells the pituitary to release growth hormone in its natural pulses.

What studies show

  • In peopleIt was approved in the 1990s to test and treat growth hormone deficiency in children.
  • In peopleSmall studies in older adults showed higher growth hormone and IGF-1, with mixed effects on body composition.

What we don’t know yet

  • Whether it slows aging or builds meaningful muscle in healthy adults.

Side effects and cautions

  • Injection-site reactions, flushing and headache.

Is it legal?

The brand-name drug was withdrawn for business reasons, not safety. Today it is made by compounding pharmacies with a prescription.
