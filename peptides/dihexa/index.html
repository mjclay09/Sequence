Dihexa, simplified.

Also called PNB-0408

Early research

An experimental compound that boosted memory and brain connections in rats. It has never been tested in people, and its mechanism raises safety questions.

HxYIAhx
Size
Modified 3-amino-acid peptide
How it is taken
Oral or injection
Status
Research compound
Available sizes
Vials: 10 mg

What is it?

Dihexa is built from a fragment of angiotensin. In the lab it boosts a growth signal called HGF, which helps brain cells form new connections.

What studies show

  • In animalsRats with memory problems performed better on maze tests.
  • In the labIncreased the number of connections between brain cells in lab dishes.

What we don’t know yet

  • Effects and safety in people. There are no human trials.

Side effects and cautions

  • The HGF growth signal it boosts is also involved in cancer growth, which is a serious theoretical concern.
  • No human safety data.

Is it legal?

Not FDA-approved for human use.
