Peptides Simplified/Melanotan 1

Skin & hair

Melanotan 1, simplified.

Also called Afamelanotide, Scenesse

Approved Rx

A lab-made copy of the hormone that makes your skin tan. An implant version is approved for a rare disease that makes sunlight painful.

SYSNleEHdFRWGKPV
Size
13 amino acids
How it is taken
Implant (approved form) or injection
Status
FDA-approved for one rare disease
Available sizes
Vials: 10 mg

What is it?

It copies alpha-MSH, the signal that tells skin cells to make the brown pigment melanin. Unlike Melanotan 2, it focuses on skin pigment and has fewer effects elsewhere.

What studies show

  • In peopleIn people with erythropoietic protoporphyria (EPP), the implant let them spend more pain-free time in sunlight.
  • In peopleIt darkens skin in healthy volunteers.

What we don’t know yet

  • Long-term safety of tanning use in healthy people.

Side effects and cautions

  • Nausea, headache and flushing.
  • Can darken moles. Have new or changing moles checked.
  • Research vials are not the approved implant.

Is it legal?

The implant is FDA-approved for EPP only. Research-grade vials are not approved.
