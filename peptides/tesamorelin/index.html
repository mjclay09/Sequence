Tesamorelin, simplified.

Also called Egrifta

Approved Rx

A prescription peptide that tells your body to make more of its own growth hormone. It is approved to shrink deep belly fat in one specific group of patients.

Daily injection
Status
FDA-approved for one use

What is it?

Your brain releases a signal called GHRH that tells the pituitary gland to make growth hormone. Tesamorelin is a longer-lasting copy of that signal.

What studies show

  • In peopleIn people with HIV-related belly fat, it cut deep (visceral) belly fat by about 15% over six months.
  • In peopleEarly studies suggest it may reduce liver fat.

What we don’t know yet

  • How well it works for general fat loss in healthy people.
  • Belly fat tends to come back after stopping.

Side effects and cautions

  • Joint pain, swelling and injection-site reactions.
  • Can raise blood sugar.

Is it legal?

FDA-approved for HIV-related abdominal fat. Other uses are off-label.
