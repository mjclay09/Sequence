Peptides Simplified/GHK-Cu

Skin & hair · Healing & injury · Aging & longevity

GHK-Cu, simplified.

Also called Copper peptide

Human studies

A tiny copper-carrying peptide found in your blood. In skin creams, it has real human data for firmer, smoother skin.

GHKCu
Size
3 amino acids + copper
How it is taken
Cream or serum (also injected)
Status
Cosmetic ingredient

What is it?

GHK grabs onto copper and helps deliver it to cells. Your levels drop as you age. In skin, it signals cells to make collagen and repair damage.

What studies show

  • In peopleIn small studies of face creams, it improved skin firmness, thickness and fine lines over about 12 weeks.
  • In the labIn lab studies, it switched on genes linked to repair and switched down genes linked to inflammation.
  • In animalsIt sped up wound healing in animals.

What we don’t know yet

  • Whether injecting it does anything that creams do not.
  • How much it helps hair growth. The evidence is thin.

Side effects and cautions

  • Creams are generally well tolerated.
  • Injected versions are much less studied.

Is it legal?

Legal as a cosmetic ingredient. Injectable forms are included in the 2026 HHS compounding plan.
