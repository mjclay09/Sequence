Semax, simplified.

Also called ACTH(4-10) analog

Human studies

A nasal spray peptide from Russia used for focus and stroke recovery.

MEHFPGP
Size
7 amino acids
How it is taken
Nasal spray
Status
Research compound (Rx in Russia)

What is it?

Semax is built from a piece of the stress hormone ACTH, changed so it no longer affects stress hormones but still acts on the brain.

What studies show

  • In peopleIt is prescribed in Russia for stroke recovery and certain brain conditions, based mostly on Russian clinical studies.
  • In animalsIn animals, it raised BDNF, a protein that supports brain cell growth.

What we don’t know yet

  • There are almost no large Western trials.
  • Whether it improves focus in healthy people.

Side effects and cautions

  • Nasal irritation is the most common complaint.

Is it legal?

Not FDA-approved. Included in the 2026 HHS compounding plan and reviewed by an FDA advisory committee in July 2026.
