Peptides Simplified/PT-141

Libido

PT-141, simplified.

Also called Bremelanotide, Vyleesi

Approved Rx

A prescription peptide for low sexual desire that works on the brain, not blood flow.

NleDHFRWK
Size
7 amino acids (ring-shaped)
How it is taken
Injection as needed
Status
FDA-approved for one use

What is it?

PT-141 acts on melanocortin receptors in the brain, which are involved in desire. That is different from drugs like Viagra, which work on blood flow.

What studies show

  • In peopleIn two large trials of premenopausal women with low desire, it modestly improved desire and reduced distress compared with placebo.

What we don’t know yet

  • How well it works in men. Studies are small.

Side effects and cautions

  • Nausea is common, affecting about 4 in 10 people in trials.
  • It can briefly raise blood pressure.
  • Repeated use can darken patches of skin.

Is it legal?

FDA-approved for premenopausal women with low sexual desire. Other uses are off-label.
