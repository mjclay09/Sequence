What is it?

Kisspeptin sits at the top of the hormone chain that controls testosterone, estrogen and fertility.

What studies show

  • In peopleIn people, it raises reproductive hormones and has been used to help trigger egg release in IVF studies.
  • In peopleBrain-scan studies found it boosted brain activity linked to attraction and desire.
