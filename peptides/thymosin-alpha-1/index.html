What is it?

The thymus is where some immune cells learn their job. Thymosin alpha-1 helps those T cells mature and respond.

What studies show

  • In peopleIt is approved in many countries to support treatment of hepatitis B and C.
  • In peopleIt has been studied alongside vaccines and in serious infections, with mixed results.

What we don’t know yet
